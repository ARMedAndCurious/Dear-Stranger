# Dear, Stranger. 📖
*A little advice. From someone you've never met.*

## The Problem

Sometimes, the words we need most come from someone who has already been through what we're experiencing. But meaningful advice often gets lost in social feeds, and language, typing preferences, or reading difficulties can create barriers to sharing and receiving it.

## The Solution

**Dear, Stranger.** is a multilingual, anonymous advice notebook where strangers can share meaningful words with one another.

Users can create personal notebooks, invite contributions through shareable links, or explore public notebooks about love, family, friendship, careers, and life.

Every approved contribution becomes a page inside an interactive hardcover diary, creating a quieter, more personal way to experience advice.

## How AI Makes It Possible

- **Gnani Prisma v3:** Converts spoken advice in supported Indian languages into editable text.
- **Google Cloud Translation:** Allows readers to experience advice in their preferred supported language.
- **Gnani Timbre v2.5:** Enables listeners to hear diary entries aloud, with a compatible browser voice as fallback when available.
- **Groq-assisted moderation:** Helps screen submissions before publication, alongside automated checks and notebook-owner review.

**Speak → Transcribe → Moderate → Publish → Translate → Listen**

Users can also type advice directly or read the original text without translation.

## Key Features

- **Anonymous contributions:** Share advice without creating an account.
- **Personal notebooks:** Create a notebook and invite others to contribute.
- **Genre-based discovery:** Find public notebooks by topic or search.
- **Interactive diary:** Read advice through a hardcover notebook with animated page turning.
- **A Page for You:** Discover a random piece of approved advice.
- **Accessible reading:** Read Aloud, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion support.

## Technology

React, Vite, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Gnani AI, Google Cloud Translation, and Groq.

## Why It Matters

Dear, Stranger. explores a simple possibility: someone can speak advice in one language, and a stranger can discover, read, or listen to those words in another.

The goal is to make sharing experiences feel more personal, inclusive, and accessible—one diary page at a time.
